## What the tables claim

Evidence-only. The "broader-resource supported coverage" rows are a **new,
separately labeled analysis** that estimates how much of the EXP-001 unresolved
vocabulary is attested in alternative resources (`isv.dic` exact surface /
`interslavicfreq` exact surface) — they do not recalculate the historical
A/B/C reports and are not a validity claim. See
`docs/RESOURCE_POLICY.md` for interpretation.
